About automatic capture

Apple and Google don't let apps read your personal texts or call history, so fully automatic tracking works through a business phone number (OpenPhone, RingCentral, Twilio — their APIs report every call's duration and every text thread) and a connected email account (Gmail / Outlook APIs). Calls and emails matched to a client then log themselves. This prototype uses one-tap quick logging so the workflow is real today; the integrations bolt onto the same data model later.
