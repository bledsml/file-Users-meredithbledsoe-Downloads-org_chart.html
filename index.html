How these estimates work & data sources

These are estimates, not live quotes. Uber, Lyft, and Waymo do not offer a free public price API, so this app models each service's published San Francisco pricing structure (base fare, booking/service fee, per-mile and per-minute rates, minimum fare) and applies your selected surge and time-of-day multipliers.

  • Distance is computed from the two map points (haversine) with a road-winding factor.
  • Trip time assumes ~18 mph average SF city speed.
  • Actual prices vary with real-time demand, traffic, promotions, and tolls.

To wire in live prices, replace estimateFare() in the code with calls to each provider's authenticated pricing endpoint (requires a partner API key).
